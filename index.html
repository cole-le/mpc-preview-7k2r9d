Who we are

Two companies, one founder, over twenty years in the market

REA Immo S.A.R.L.

Real Estate Africa Immo. A multinational Congolese brokerage, promotion and development company in Commune de la Gombe, Kinshasa. Owns the Maluku land, sells residential plots today, and runs an agent academy that certifies agents only after three real transactions.

Entrepreneurs & Investors Group LLC

North Carolina company founded in 2001, active in real estate, IT and telecom, agriculture and critical minerals. The base rate accrues while the project is being built and before sales revenue arrives. It is held, not forfeited.

The accrued amount is released across the four quarters, with the year 4 entitlement. At Tier III that is USD 26,250 a quarter.

The doubled rate, paid quarterly to the end of the term. Capital returns in full in the final year.

How it works

Five steps, most of them on your phone

  1. RegisterName, email and country on the portal. Summary and tiers sent straight away.
  2. VerifyIdentity, address, eligibility and source of funds, reviewed by the EIG LLC team.
  3. ReviewFull documents, return calculator, project data room, and a call with a principal.
  4. SubscribeChoose a tier and amount, sign electronically.
  5. Transfer and closingThree business days from subscription. Bank transfer to the EIG LLC account in the United States only, never to the DR Congo. Receipt confirmed, instrument issued.

EIG LLC will never change its bank details by email or message. Verify any change by telephone before paying.

Protection and transparency

What a careful participant can check

Verified participants only

Every participant is identity-checked and screened against sanctions lists before anything is signed.

Documents before signature

Subscription agreement, participation instrument and risk disclosure, reviewed with your own adviser.

A data room, not a promise

Title, company and project documents available to verified participants, every view logged.

Funds to one account

You transfer only to the EIG LLC account named in your instructions, in the United States. EIG LLC then moves capital to MPC-1, which holds the project escrow in the DR Congo.

What secures the participation

MPC-1 issues a corporate guarantee to EIG LLC, which EIG LLC provides to client-participants. The instrument is still to be supplied.

Open note in the deck. The instrument is still to be supplied.

Reporting

Quarterly and annual statements, in the same way you receive a bank statement. There is no annual assembly to attend. During years 1 to 3 the quarterly statement shows the return accruing, even though nothing is paid yet.

The programme deck, page 14.

Risks, plainly

What could go wrong, stated up front

Not guaranteed

The return is an obligation of the issuer, not a guarantee.

Country

Political, legal and enforcement risk in the DR Congo.

Construction

Delays and cost increases are possible.

Sales

Slower sales make the higher rate harder to meet.

Currency

The programme is paid in US dollars throughout. If your own currency is not the dollar, movements between them affect what you receive.

Long term

Eight to twelve years, with limited or no early exit.

This is a high risk, high return programme, and it is presented as one. Safeguards and mitigation measures are in place across the group behind it, and they are set out in detail on request. The full Risk Disclosure is provided before any subscription and should be read with an independent adviser.

What a site and a LifeBook add to a PDF deck

  • Every figure on the page can be tapped to show where it comes from: the deck page it is printed on, or the public page that states it. A figure with an open drafting note shows that note beside it, in the deck's own words.
  • The portal is one tap from every page, at the address the owner confirms. Today the deck prints eigportal.com, which opens an unrelated website; a link is checked once and then correct everywhere.
  • The risk page and the "Important information" notice are present on every view of the site and at the end of the LifeBook, word for word, not only on the last page of a PDF.
  • One link to send in place of an attachment. It stays private and unlisted, and when the draft changes the same link shows the new wording.
  • The LifeBook follows the deck page by page on a phone: the land, the tier table, the three years with nothing paid, the five steps and the leadership page, each readable without zooming.
